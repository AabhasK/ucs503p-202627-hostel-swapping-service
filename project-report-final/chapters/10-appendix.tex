\chapter{Reproducing the Evaluation}
\label{app:repro}

The frozen-cohort figures in Table~\ref{tab:demo-k} come from the demo
endpoint, run without a database:

\begin{lstlisting}[language=bash]
pip install -e "./code[dev]"
uvicorn hostelswap.api:app
curl "http://127.0.0.1:8000/api/demo?k=1"    # Hungarian only
curl "http://127.0.0.1:8000/api/demo?k=50"   # live default
\end{lstlisting}

The chain-cap benchmark uses cohorts from
\texttt{hostelswap.synth.generator} with seeds 1--10 and 300 students,
$K = 200$, and applies caps of 5, 8 and 10 to the output of
\texttt{recommend\_chains}. Its figures and charts are in
\texttt{benchmarks/chain-cap-deck.html}.
